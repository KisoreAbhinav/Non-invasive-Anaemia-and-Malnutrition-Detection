# WHO growth-reference data

These tab-separated LMS reference tables are copied verbatim from repositories
maintained by the World Health Organization so Z-scores can be calculated fully
offline.

- WHO `anthro` commit `b776d8a12b1c97369c748b561159fd2ec4f4db58`:
  `weianthro.txt`, `lenanthro.txt`, `wflanthro.txt`, `wfhanthro.txt`
- WHO `anthroplus` commit `7cfcdb39026e9a55de55732bc3cf14c82261bcf7`:
  `bfawho2007.txt`, `hfawho2007.txt`, `wfawho2007.txt`

Sources:

- https://github.com/WorldHealthOrganization/anthro
- https://github.com/WorldHealthOrganization/anthroplus

For children 5–12, weight-for-height is not a WHO 2007 indicator; the correct
proportional-growth indicator is BMI-for-age. Weight-for-age is only reported
through 10 years, matching the WHO reference's defined range.
